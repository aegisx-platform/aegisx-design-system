How this works

Each cell's color and background are resolved through getComputedStyle. Relative luminance follows the WCAG 2.1 formula; contrast ratio is (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05). Thresholds: AA normal text ≥ 4.5, AAA ≥ 7, non-text ≥ 3.
